The ISC2 chapter for Arkansas

ISC2 Arkansas is the official ISC2 chapter for the state. We bring together people who work in cybersecurity, or want to, from across the Natural State to learn from each other and build a stronger security community here.

Meetings are open to everyone, and you don't need an ISC2 certification to join.

Join the LinkedIn group

Meetings

The chapter meets online on the second Friday of each month at 2:00 PM Central, on Google Meet. We also hold in-person socials and show up at regional conferences through the year.

Meeting links and event announcements go out through the chapter LinkedIn group. Join the group to get them.

Membership

To join, fill in the membership application (PDF) and email it to membership@isc2arkansas.org. Please read the chapter membership guidelines and the ISC2 Code of Ethics first.

Do I need to be a member to attend a meeting?

No. Anyone interested in cybersecurity in Arkansas and the surrounding area is welcome at chapter meetings.

Do I need an ISC2 certification to join?

No. The chapter is open to anyone interested in cybersecurity, certified or not.

Are there dues?

The chapter does not charge dues at this time.

About the chapter

Vision

To be the cybersecurity center for the state of Arkansas, where both active and aspiring IT security professionals can gather to support and learn from one another.

Mission

To serve our members by providing opportunities for continuous education across multiple cybersecurity domains.
